1. What is free and what is paid

Weekly LACE runs are free and open to anyone. No membership, no ticket, no sign-up. Turn up at the listed time and place and run.

Paid products are separate:

  • Founding membership — a paid tier limited to 100 places, giving priority access to experiences and member-only sessions.
  • Merchandise — House of LACE apparel, sold in limited runs.

2. Payment

All payments are processed by Stripe. Lace Company sells from the United States. The price shown at checkout is the total you pay, and we add no charges after it. Depending on where you live, your own customs or tax authority may apply import charges on physical goods, which are yours to settle. We do not receive, handle or store your card details at any point. You will see the charge on your statement under our Stripe descriptor, and Stripe emails you a receipt.

3. Right of withdrawal

If you are a consumer resident in the EU, Article 52 of the Italian Consumer Code (Codice del Consumo, Legislative Decree 206/2005) gives you 14 days to withdraw from a distance purchase without giving a reason.

Membership

The 14 days run from the date of purchase. Email us within that window and we refund in full, provided you have not already attended a members-only session or claimed a member benefit. If you have, we deduct the proportional value of what you used.

Merchandise

The 14 days run from the day the item reaches you. Return it unworn, unwashed and with tags attached, and we refund the item price. Return postage is yours unless the item arrived faulty or wrong, in which case we cover it and refund your original delivery charge too.

How to withdraw

Email customersupport@lacenapoli.com with the subject line "Withdrawal" and your order number. Refunds go back to your original payment method within 14 days of us receiving the returned item or your membership cancellation.

4. Pre-orders

Some apparel is sold as a pre-order before production. When you place one we tell you the expected dispatch window. If that window slips by more than 30 days, you can cancel for a full refund with a single email, no questions asked. If a run sells out below the minimum needed to produce it, we cancel the run and refund everyone in full.

5. Experiences and paid events

Trips, races and paid experiences are sold under their own booking terms, which we give you in writing before you pay. Because these carry non-recoverable costs booked on your behalf, the standard 14-day withdrawal right does not apply once a place is confirmed, in line with Art. 59 of the Consumer Code covering leisure services on a specific date. Cancellation terms are stated on each booking.
