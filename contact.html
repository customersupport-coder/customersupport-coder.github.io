lacenapoli.com is the official website of LACE

LACE is a running and social community founded in Naples in 2024. We run weekly, for free, in Napoli, Caserta, Milano and Salerno, and we have grown to more than 3,000 members. Everything on this site is published by the LACE team. If you have seen LACE anywhere else asking you for money, it did not come from us.
